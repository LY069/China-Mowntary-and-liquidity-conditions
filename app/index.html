The transmission gap

Both indices on one scale, with the gap between them below. When liquidity runs loose while monetary conditions stay tight, the central bank is supplying money the credit system is not passing on — the defining configuration of a balance-sheet recession.

Headline indicators

The core dashboard. Loose and tight are measured against each series' own history on the available sample, not against a neutral level. Select any row for the full series, the transmission mechanism and the ways it misleads.

Monetary conditions describe the stance of policy as it is actually transmitted to households and firms — the price and quantity of credit plus the exchange rate, over a horizon of quarters. They answer: is money cheap and available for the real economy?

Monetary condition indicators

Grouped by transmission channel. The state column signs each series so that positive always means looser.

Liquidity conditions describe the availability and price of settlement balances and short-term funding inside the financial system, over a horizon of days to weeks. They answer: can banks and non-banks fund themselves, and at what cost relative to the policy rate?

Liquidity condition indicators

Built from spreads wherever possible, so the readings measure conditions relative to the policy rate rather than the level of rates.
